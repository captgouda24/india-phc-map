      About this map

      Each dot is a Primary Health Centre from the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare's All India Health Centres Directory (as of 7 October 2016): 28,110 PHCs with usable coordinates.

      • The data is from 2016. Some PHCs have since been renamed Ayushman Arogya Mandirs, upgraded, or closed, and newer ones are missing.
      • Some pins are approximate (orange ring). They may mark a village or block centre, or a nearby facility. Search by name to find the building.
      • District names are as of 2016. Many districts have since been split.
      • Cleaning: duplicate "Andhra Pradesh Old" rows removed; swapped latitude/longitude corrected.
      • Use the layer button (top right) to switch between street and satellite maps. Basemap tiles © Esri and its data partners; boundaries are not authenticated by the Survey of India.
